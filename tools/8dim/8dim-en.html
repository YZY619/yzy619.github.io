Before you start:

Self-Assessment Tool: This is an open-source, self-administered assessment based on Jungian cognitive function theory. It is NOT a clinical diagnostic product and does NOT substitute for professional psychological counseling or career evaluation.

Independent Project: This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by The Myers-Briggs Company. It is NOT an official MBTI® product.

Nature of Results: Personality traits are fluid and context-dependent. Your scores reflect a snapshot of your cognitive preferences at this moment—not fixed labels. Results are for self-reflection only and should NOT be the sole basis for major life decisions (e.g., hiring, admissions, relationships).

Source of Questions: All questions in this assessment are independently developed by the author. They are not from any official question bank, and the quality of the questions is for reference only.

Data Privacy: All responses are processed locally in your browser/device. We do NOT collect personally identifiable information such as your name or contact details.

By clicking "Start Assessment" you acknowledge that you have read and understood the above.

Please make sure you have selected the correct language. You will not be able to switch languages during the assessment — doing so will erase all your responses.
